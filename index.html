We are Northwest Arkansas' home to Riichi Mahjong

ORC (Ozark Riichi Club) is dedicated to playing, teaching, and growing interest in Japanese-style (Riichi) Mahjong in Northwest Arkansas. We welcome new beginners, experienced competitors, and those who are accustomed to different styles of mahjong!

FAQs

Can I come if I don't know how to play riichi?

Yes! We welcome all levels, from beginners to experts. We teach at every meet-up!

Do I need to bring anything?

No, we will provide all the materials, so you can show up and be ready to learn. If you would like us to know you will be there, please message us on Instagram or join our Discord.

How much does it cost?

All our events are free.
